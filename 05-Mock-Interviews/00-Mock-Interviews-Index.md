# Mock Interviews Index

Mock interviews are where prep becomes performance. They expose the gap between "I know this" and "I can do this under pressure with someone watching."

## Why they matter

- timing and pacing (45-min coding, 45-min design, 15-min behavioral)
- talking through your thinking out loud
- handling edge cases and follow-ups you didn't anticipate
- calibrating self-assessment against a real rubric

## Core skills

- structured coding walkthroughs (approach → code → test)
- clear tradeoff storytelling in system design
- STAR stories told start-to-finish with numbers
- honest post-mortems: top 3 gaps + top 3 strengths
